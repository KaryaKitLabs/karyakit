What Is a Percentage?

A percentage expresses a value as a part of 100. The word percentage means “per hundred” and is commonly written using the % symbol.

Percentages are used for exam marks, discounts, price changes, interest rates, statistics and many everyday calculations. A percentage calculator helps you work out these values without doing the arithmetic manually.

Percentage Formulas

1. Percentage of a number

Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

Example: 20% of 500 = (20 ÷ 100) × 500 = 100.

2. What percentage is one number of another?

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Example: 25 out of 200 = (25 ÷ 200) × 100 = 12.5%.

3. Percentage increase or decrease

Percentage change = ((New value − Original value) ÷ Original value) × 100

A positive result means an increase. A negative result means a decrease. The percentage change calculator displays the direction as well as the size of the change.

4. Marks percentage

Marks percentage = (Marks obtained ÷ Maximum marks) × 100

Example: 425 marks out of 500 = 85%.

5. Reverse percentage

Original before increase = Final value ÷ (1 + Rate ÷ 100)
Original before decrease = Final value ÷ (1 − Rate ÷ 100)

For a decrease, the rate must be less than 100% to calculate a positive original value.

How to Calculate Your Exam Marks Percentage

  1. Add the marks you obtained in the subjects you want to include.
  2. Add the maximum possible marks for those same subjects.
  3. Enter both totals in the Marks Percentage Calculator above.
  4. Select Calculate marks percentage to see your result.

For example, if your total is 425 out of 500, your percentage is 85%. Follow your school or examination board's rules about which subjects and marks should be included in an official result.

CGPA to Percentage Conversion

There is no single conversion multiplier that applies to every school, university or grading system. Some institutions use a multiplier, while others publish a different formula.

The converter above lets you enter your institution's multiplier. Its initial value of 9.5 is only an editable example, not a guarantee of the correct formula for your course or marksheet.

For an official application, admission or examination form, use the conversion method specified by the institution that issued your CGPA.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage?

To find what percentage one number is of another, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. To find a percentage of a number, divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number.

How do I calculate my exam marks percentage?

Add the marks obtained, divide by the total maximum marks, and multiply by 100. Use the same subjects in both totals.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value, and multiply by 100.

How do I calculate percentage decrease?

Subtract the new value from the original value, divide the difference by the original value, and multiply by 100.

Can I calculate a percentage backwards?

Yes. Enter the final value and the percentage increase or decrease in the reverse percentage calculator to estimate the original value.

Is there one universal CGPA-to-percentage formula?

No. Conversion rules vary by institution and grading system. Check your institution's official instructions and enter its multiplier in the converter.
