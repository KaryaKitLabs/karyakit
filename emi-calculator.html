What Is an EMI Calculator?

An EMI calculator estimates the fixed monthly instalment you may pay to repay a loan over a chosen period. EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It combines a principal component, which repays the borrowed amount, and an interest component charged by the lender.

You can use this loan EMI calculator to estimate repayments for a home loan, car loan or personal loan. Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure to see the estimated monthly EMI, total interest and total repayment.

EMI Formula

The standard reducing-balance EMI formula is:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

Where:

  • P is the principal loan amount.
  • r is the monthly interest rate, calculated as the annual rate divided by 12 and then by 100.
  • n is the total number of monthly instalments.

When the interest rate is 0%, the EMI is the principal divided by the number of instalments. The repayment schedule rounds each instalment to the nearest paise, so the final instalment may be adjusted slightly to clear the remaining balance.

What Affects Your Monthly EMI?

  • Loan amount: A larger principal generally results in a higher EMI when the rate and tenure stay the same.
  • Interest rate: A higher interest rate generally increases the EMI and the total interest payable.
  • Loan tenure: A longer tenure generally lowers the monthly EMI but can increase the total interest paid.

A home loan EMI calculator, car loan EMI calculator and personal loan EMI calculator use the same basic formula. The rate, fees, repayment rules and other terms depend on the specific lender and loan product.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does EMI stand for?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the regular monthly payment made towards repaying a loan, including principal and interest.

How is monthly EMI calculated?

Monthly EMI is commonly calculated using the principal, monthly interest rate and total number of monthly instalments. This calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula.

Can I use this as a home loan EMI calculator?

Yes. Enter your home loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure to estimate the monthly instalment. Check the lender's offer and fees for the actual repayment terms.

Can I calculate car loan or personal loan EMI?

Yes. The calculator can estimate EMI for a car loan, personal loan or another loan that follows a monthly reducing-balance repayment structure.

Does a longer loan tenure reduce EMI?

A longer tenure generally reduces the monthly EMI for the same principal and interest rate, but it can increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Does this calculator include processing fees or insurance?

No. The estimate uses the loan principal, annual interest rate and tenure. Processing fees, insurance, taxes, late charges, prepayments and rate changes are not included.

Is this EMI calculator free?

Yes. You can use the calculator without creating an account. Calculations are performed in your browser.
