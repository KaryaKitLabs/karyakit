Contact KaryaKit

Found a bug, have a suggestion, or want to report an issue? You can contact the project through its public GitHub repository.

Report a bug or suggest a feature

Visit KaryaKit on GitHub — Issues and open an issue. Please do not post passwords, private documents, phone numbers or other sensitive personal information.

Before reporting

  • Tell us which tool you were using.
  • Describe what you expected and what happened.
  • Include your device and browser type if relevant.
  • Do not attach private images or documents.
