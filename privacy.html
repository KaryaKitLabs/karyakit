Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how the KaryaKit website handles information. KaryaKit is an early-stage utility website. Features and this policy may change as the service develops.

Information you enter

KaryaKit does not currently require account registration. Calculator values and text entered into the Word Counter are processed in your browser. The current app does not provide a feature for us to receive or store those entries.

Images and PDF files

The Image Compressor, Image Resizer and PDF Merger are designed to process the files you select locally in your browser. The current app code does not intentionally upload those selected files to a KaryaKit application server. Avoid using a shared or untrusted device for sensitive documents.

Camera permission

The QR Code Scanner can request camera access if you choose to scan using your camera. The browser displays a permission prompt. You can deny or revoke camera access in your browser settings; image-based scanning is also available.

Third-party libraries and hosting

Some features load open-source JavaScript libraries from cdnjs, a third-party content delivery network. When your browser requests those files, the CDN may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser request data under its own policies. The website is hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure, which may process technical data and security logs as part of delivering and protecting the site. Review the relevant providers’ privacy documentation for their practices.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current KaryaKit app does not intentionally use advertising or analytics trackers. Hosting or security infrastructure may use technically necessary mechanisms. If analytics, advertising, accounts or payments are added later, this policy will be updated before those features are introduced.

Children

KaryaKit is a general-purpose utility website and is not specifically directed at children. Do not submit sensitive personal information through any future contact or feedback feature.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as KaryaKit changes. For questions or privacy concerns, use the contact route described on the Contact page.
