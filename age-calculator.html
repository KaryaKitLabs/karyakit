What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates the time between your date of birth and a selected date. It displays your age in completed years, months and days.

Instead of manually counting calendar days and months, you can enter your birth date and get an age calculation in seconds.

How to Use This Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Select the date on which you want to calculate your age.
  3. Click the Calculate Age button.
  4. View your age in years, months and days.
  5. Check your total elapsed days and next birthday information.

How Is Your Age Calculated?

Your age is calculated by comparing your date of birth with the selected calculation date.

The calculator counts completed calendar months from your birth date. If the target month does not contain your birth day, it uses the last day of that month for the monthly anniversary. The remaining days are counted from that anniversary to the selected date.

Total elapsed days are calculated from the difference between the two calendar dates, including leap days.

For birthdays on 29 February, this calculator treats 28 February as the birthday in non-leap years. Legal age and administrative rules may differ by location.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I calculate my exact age?

Enter your date of birth, select the calculation date and click Calculate Age. The tool displays your age in years, months and days.

Can I calculate my age in months and days?

Yes. This age calculator displays completed years, remaining months and remaining days separately.

Can I calculate my age on a past date?

Yes. Select a past date in the Calculate Age On field to find how old you were on that date.

Does this calculator include leap years?

Yes. The total elapsed days include leap days. The age calculation also accounts for the length of each calendar month.

Is this age calculator free?

Yes. You can use this calculator for free without creating an account.

Is my date of birth stored?

The calculation runs in your browser. This page does not send the dates you enter to a KaryaKit calculation service.
