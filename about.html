About KaryaKit

Everyday tools. One simple place.

KaryaKit is a lightweight collection of practical tools designed to make common digital tasks easier. It brings calculators, QR utilities, image tools, text tools and PDF utilities together in one place.

What you can do

  • Calculate age, percentages, loan EMIs and GST.
  • Generate and scan QR codes.
  • Compress and resize images.
  • Count words and merge PDF files.

Simple by design

KaryaKit is designed to work in a modern web browser without requiring an account. Tools that process selected images or PDFs are designed to work locally in your browser; see the Privacy Policy for important details about third-party libraries and hosting.

Our aim

To keep useful everyday utilities easy to find, straightforward to use and accessible across desktop and mobile devices.
