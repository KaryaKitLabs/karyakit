What Is a GST Calculator?

A GST calculator is a tool for working out the Goods and Services Tax amount on a price. It can calculate tax added to a pre-tax amount or extract the tax component from a price that already includes GST. This page performs the arithmetic using the rate you enter; it does not decide which GST rate legally applies to a product or service.

How to Use This GST Calculator

  1. Choose Add GST if your amount is before tax, or Remove GST if it already includes GST.
  2. Enter the amount in rupees.
  3. Select a GST rate or enter another rate when applicable.
  4. Choose intra-state or inter-state supply to display the tax split.
  5. Click Calculate GST to view the taxable value, tax and total amount.

GST Calculation Formulas

When adding GST to an amount before tax:

GST amount = Original amount × GST rate ÷ 100

Price including GST = Original amount + GST amount

When removing GST from a price that already includes tax:

Taxable value = Inclusive amount ÷ (1 + GST rate ÷ 100)

GST amount = Inclusive amount − Taxable value

For an intra-state supply, the GST amount is generally split between CGST and SGST/UTGST. For an inter-state supply, IGST is generally calculated on the taxable value. The calculator shows a simple equal CGST/SGST split for the intra-state arithmetic.

Example: Adding GST

Suppose the pre-tax price is ₹1,000 and the selected GST rate is 18%. The GST amount is ₹180, so the total price including GST is ₹1,180. For an intra-state calculation, the illustrative split is CGST ₹90 and SGST ₹90. For an inter-state calculation, IGST is ₹180.

Example: Removing GST

Suppose the price including GST is ₹1,180 and the selected rate is 18%. The taxable value is ₹1,000 and the GST included in the price is ₹180. The result is calculated by dividing the inclusive amount by 1.18.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate GST on an amount?

Multiply the pre-tax amount by the GST rate and divide by 100. Add the result to the original amount to get the price including GST.

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Divide the GST-inclusive amount by 1 plus the GST rate expressed as a decimal. Subtract the taxable value from the inclusive amount to find the GST component.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

For a typical intra-state taxable supply, CGST and SGST/UTGST are applied together. For an inter-state taxable supply, IGST generally applies. The place-of-supply rules determine which treatment is appropriate.

Does this calculator tell me the correct GST rate for a product?

No. It calculates tax using the rate you select. The applicable rate depends on the product or service classification, HSN/SAC details and current rules. Confirm the rate from an authoritative tax source or a qualified tax professional.

Is this GST calculator free?

Yes. You can use this calculator in your browser without creating an account.

Are my amounts uploaded or stored?

The calculation is performed in your browser. This page does not send the amounts you enter to a KaryaKit calculation server.
